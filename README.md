Larry Niven's full **Ringworld** tetralogy — a hard-SF pivot from the
[muster rolls](https://github.com/DavidWise01/bridge-burners) that came before. The Land,
the River, the Despiser were sorted by virtue and vice. **Known Space doesn't work that
way.** Here biology is destiny — the Puppeteer's cowardice, the Kzin's aggression, the
human's bred luck, the Pak Protector's bloodline-logic are *species traits*, not moral
choices — so this roll sorts the cast by **kind**, and sets the artifact itself apart.

> A band of matter a million miles wide and six hundred million long, spun around a sun
> for gravity. Three million times the surface of the Earth. **The idea is the hero.**

## The roll

### 🜨 Humanity — Sol's wanderers, and the luck they were bred for
- **Louis Wu** — 200 and bored of it; explorer and peacemaker; the eyes through which we see the Ring.
- **Teela Brown** — bred for total luck; on the Ring her luck stops protecting her and starts *using* her.

### ⟁ Pierson's Puppeteers — cowardice raised to statecraft
- **Nessus** — the Puppeteer who organized the expedition; "insane" by his kind's standard, i.e. brave enough to leave the Fleet.
- **The Hindmost** — leader-in-exile; pulls strings from safety; his kind bred human luck itself.

### 🐅 The Kzinti — aggression as a species strategy
- **Speaker-to-Animals (Chmeee)** — Kzin warrior-diplomat; earns a full name, the rarest honor among felines who lost four wars to monkeys.
- **The Patriarchy** — the empire of appetite, tempered only by the wars it kept losing.

### 🌳 The Pak Protectors — intelligence in service of kin · the builders
- **Tunesmith** — the Ringworld Protector who takes the failing Ring's fate in hand; brilliant, cold, beyond human scale.
- **The Pak** — tree-of-life makes Protectors: deathless, superhumanly smart, caring for nothing but bloodline. The builders, and the warning.

### ◇ The Hominids of the Ring — humanity's cousins, gone strange
- **Halrloprillalar (Prill)** — ship's woman of the fallen City Builders; guide and keeper of old knowledge.
- **The Night People (Ghouls)** — eaters of the dead and the Ring's hidden information network; the species that actually runs the place.
- **The Breeds of the Ring** — a thousand hominid species across three million Earths: Machine People, Hanging People, Grass Giants, Vampires, Reds.

### ◯ The Ringworld — *set apart, the artifact-protagonist*
- **The Ringworld** — scrith a million miles wide and six hundred million long; shadow squares for night, rim walls a thousand miles high; built by Pak Protectors, failing slowly, worth a whole genre of wonder.
